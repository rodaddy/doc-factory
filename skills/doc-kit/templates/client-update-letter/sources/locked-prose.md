# Locked prose — client-update-letter (FALLBACK SKELETON)

Each locked paragraph gets a number. **Those numbers are the provenance
handles**: every document built from this template cites them on its
verification page, and `source-record.md` says which source document each one
came out of.

For this skeleton the answer is always the same and always disappointing:
rTech wrote it, not the customer. That is why it fails the refusal gate.

## What this looks like on a real template

Same table, but every "Came from" cell names one of the customer's own
documents and its date — and the locked wording is theirs, word for word,
never rewritten or improved. That is the whole difference between this kit and
a letter generator, and it is visible right here in this file.
