# Start Here — Doc Factory

You have a Doc Factory zip. Here is how to get it working, on any device. Pick
your device, do the three quick steps, and you're done.

## On an iPhone or iPad

1. Open the **Files** app and find the zip you downloaded. **Tap it** — it
   unzips into a folder with the same name.
2. Open that folder and tap **`installer.md`** to preview it. Tap the **Share**
   icon and send it into your AI app (the Claude or ChatGPT app), or open the
   file, select all the text, and copy it.
3. In your AI app, paste it (or the shared file) and send the message below.

## On Windows

1. **Right-click** the zip → **Extract All** → **Extract**. A folder opens.
2. Open that folder and find **`installer.md`**.
3. Drag **`installer.md`** into your AI app or website (or open it in Notepad,
   select all, copy, and paste into the AI), then send the message below.

## On a Mac

1. **Double-click** the zip. A folder appears next to it.
2. Open that folder and find **`installer.md`**.
3. Drag **`installer.md`** into your AI app or website (or open it, select all,
   copy, and paste into the AI), then send the message below.

## The one message to send your AI

> **Send that file to your AI and say: install doc factory.**
