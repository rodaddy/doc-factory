# What Kowalczyk Title Agency sent

Deirdre Kowalczyk runs a six-person title agency in Islandia and closes about
forty residential deals a month. Asked for the last real "clear to close"
letter she sent, she pasted this one out of Word, real names and all.

Excerpted — the parts that matter to this example.

---

> **KOWALCZYK TITLE AGENCY LLC**
> 1800 Motor Parkway, Suite 210, Islandia, NY 11749
> (631) 555-0148 | closings@kowalczyktitle.com
>
> October 14, 2026
>
> Ronald Petrosino, Esq.
> Petrosino & Hale LLP
> 88 Froehlich Farm Blvd, Woodbury, NY 11797
>
> **Re: Premises 42 Oakleigh Lane, Massapequa Park, NY 11762 — Title No.
> KTA-26-4471**
>
> Dear Mr. Petrosino:
>
> We have completed our examination of title to the above-referenced premises
> and are pleased to advise that title is clear to close subject only to the
> items set forth below.
>
> Our search covers the period from the last insurable conveyance of record
> through the date of this letter. Any instrument recorded after the date of
> this letter and prior to the recording of the insured instrument is not
> covered by this letter and will require a continuation search, **which we
> will run at no additional charge within ten days of closing.**
>
> The following items remain open and must be resolved at or prior to closing:
>
> 1. Satisfaction of the mortgage recorded in Liber 22874 page 331 in favor of
>    Nationstar Mortgage LLC in the original amount of $310,000.00.
> 2. Payment of the open water charge in the amount of $412.86 to the
>    Massapequa Water District.
> 3. Delivery of an executed and notarized affidavit of title from the seller
>    in the form annexed to the title report.
>
> Our premium for the fee policy in the amount of $625,000.00 is $3,142.00,
> calculated at the rate promulgated by the Title Insurance Rate Service
> Association and filed with the New York State Department of Financial
> Services.
>
> Kindly forward the closer's package no later than forty-eight hours prior to
> the scheduled closing so that we may prepare the closing statement and
> disbursement sheet. Wire instructions will be transmitted separately and by
> telephone confirmation only; we do not accept changed wire instructions by
> email under any circumstances.
>
> Very truly yours,
>
> Deirdre Kowalczyk, Licensed Title Agent
> KOWALCZYK TITLE AGENCY LLC

---

**Why this document is in the examples.** It is a genuinely good letter. It
sails through the thin-template gate — 221 words of locked prose, 29% variable.
Nothing in it is careless. And there are two sentences in it that must never be
locked and one that absolutely should be, and telling them apart is the whole
example.
