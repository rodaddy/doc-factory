# What they sent

**Business:** Coastal Grade Landscaping & Hardscape LLC, Bay Shore. Nine
trucks, commercial and high-end residential.
**Owner:** Nick Petrakis.
**What he handed over:** three client update letters he had sent over the past
year, pasted out of Word, with the client names still in them. His words: "I
send some version of this every month to whoever's got a job running. I
rewrite it every time and it takes me an hour I don't have."

Letter he pointed at as the best one:

---

October 9, 2025

Marisa Coyne
Sagamore Property Group
119 Broadhollow Road, Melville, NY 11747

Re: Courtyard hardscape restoration, 119 Broadhollow

Dear Marisa,

I am writing to give you an update on the courtyard hardscape restoration at
119 Broadhollow. The crew has been on site since the 22nd and we are broadly
on schedule.

What we have completed

Demolition of the failed bluestone is finished and hauled off. The base has
been re-graded and compacted, and we have corrected the drainage fall away
from the building entrance that was causing the ponding you saw in the spring.
The new bluestone is on site and staged.

What is happening next

Setting begins Monday, weather permitting. We expect the field to be laid
within about eight working days, followed by the sawn edge detail and pointing.
We are watching the forecast; sustained rain pushes setting but does not affect
the base work already done.

What we need from you

We need your sign-off on the sawn-edge sample we left with your property
manager, and a decision on whether you want the two courtyard benches reset on
the new grade or removed. Please let us know at your convenience.

Thank you for your continued trust in Coastal Grade. If you have any questions
or would like to talk any of this through, please reach out at (631) 555-0147
or nick@coastalgradeli.com.

Sincerely,

Nick Petrakis, Owner
Coastal Grade Landscaping & Hardscape LLC

---

The other two letters were the same document to different clients. Comparing
all three is what let the kit tell locked wording from fields without asking
Nick twenty questions.
