# Worked examples — see the whole flow before you risk your own document

Four complete runs, start to finish, on realistic Long Island businesses.
Each one shows the same thing: a real document goes in, the kit finds what is
missing in it, and nothing comes out until a person accepts it.

**These are here to SHOW you the flow. They are not the install check.** Your
Doc Factory is not set up until one of YOUR OWN documents has been through it
end to end. If the installer ever offers to verify itself on one of these
instead of your document, tell it no — and it is written not to.

| # | Business | Document | What the run proves |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Coastal Grade Landscaping & Hardscape, Bay Shore | Client update letter | The urgent-ask check moves a Monday deadline out of section four and up to the top. Second run of the same client shows run-history pre-fill. |
| 2 | Vitale & Marchetti CPAs, Melville | Engagement letter | The extraction finds two real gaps in a letter they have sent for six years, and refuses to lock a paragraph with a prior client's name in it. |
| 3 | Fiore Mechanical, Ronkonkoma | Change order | The refusal fires: the source document is too thin to template, and the kit says so instead of building a skeleton. |
| 4 | Kowalczyk Title Agency, Islandia | Clear-to-close letter | A whole check list gets DROPPED because the owner says it does not apply; a one-off favour is caught one step before it becomes published policy; and the best finding in the letter is one no check list contained. |

Read them in order. Example 1 is the shortest and shows the whole shape.
Example 4 is the one to read if you want to see the kit tell the difference
between a sentence worth locking forever and a sentence that must never be.

---

## What every example contains

- `01-what-they-sent.md` — the real-shaped document the owner handed over
- `02-what-the-kit-said.md` — the conversation, including every refusal and
  every gap report, in the kit's own words
- `03-the-checks.md` — the filled `review-checks.md` for that run
- `04-the-receipt.txt` — the verification receipt the owner keeps

Example 3 has no receipt, on purpose: nothing was verified because nothing was
built. The receipts in examples 1 and 2 were produced by the kit's own
verification page (`skills/doc-kit/references/verify-page.html`), not written
by hand — that is the same code that will stamp yours.

Every one of these obeys the same rules the kit obeys. The documents are about
work getting done and hours coming back to the owner, and the owner is the one
who decides what goes out.
