# What they sent

**Business:** Vitale & Marchetti CPAs, Melville. Two partners, six staff,
mostly closely-held businesses and their owners.
**Who ran it:** Gina Vitale, managing partner.
**What she handed over:** one engagement letter, the one they have sent for
six years. Her words: "This is the letter. Everybody gets this letter. I
change the name and the fee and I send it."

Mr. Anthony Grasso
Grasso Property Management LLC

Re: Engagement for accounting and tax services

Dear Anthony,

Thank you for the opportunity to serve Grasso Property Management LLC. This
letter confirms the terms of our engagement.

We will prepare the federal and New York State income tax returns for Grasso
Property Management LLC for the year ending December 31, 2026, and provide
general accounting services including monthly bookkeeping review and quarterly
financial statement preparation.

Our fee for these services is $1,850 per month, billed on the first of the
month. Fees for services outside the scope described above will be billed at
our standard hourly rates, which are available on request.

We will rely on the accuracy and completeness of the information you provide.
You are responsible for the proper recording of transactions in the books and
records, for the safeguarding of assets, and for the substantial accuracy of
the financial records. Our engagement does not include any procedures designed
to detect fraud or illegal acts, although we will inform you of any that come
to our attention.

As we discussed following the situation with the prior bookkeeper at Grasso, we
will also perform a one-time review of the 2025 general ledger.

Please sign and return the enclosed copy of this letter to indicate your
agreement with these terms.

Very truly yours,

Gina Vitale, CPA
Managing Partner
Vitale & Marchetti CPAs
